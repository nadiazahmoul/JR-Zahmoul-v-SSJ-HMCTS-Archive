CONSOLIDATED ACCOUNTABILITY RECORD (CAR)
What this folder is

This folder is a navigation and accountability map for the repository.

It does not contain evidence.
It does not contain legal argument.

Its purpose is to help any reader understand:

what material exists,

where it is located, and

how the record has evolved over time.

Think of this folder as the index and audit spine of the archive.

What this folder is NOT

This folder does not:

duplicate documents stored elsewhere,

host raw evidence (such as emails),

argue conclusions,

summarise legal positions.

All primary material remains in its original location.

How to use this folder

If you are new to the repository:

Start here to understand the structure of the archive.

Follow the links or references to locate:

court filings,

raw evidence,

timelines and indexes.

Use the logs in this folder to see what has been added, indexed, or updated, and when.

You do not need to read everything to understand the record.

Relationship to the rest of the repository

This repository is organised in layers:

Raw evidence (e.g. original email files) is stored in the evidence folders.

Judicial materials (pleadings, orders) are stored in court record folders.

Indexes and timelines are stored separately to allow independent analysis.

The Consolidated Accountability Record points to these layers but does not replace them.

Why this folder exists

This archive is intended to be:

transparent,

auditable,

usable without explanation from the author.

The Consolidated Accountability Record ensures that:

the public record is intelligible,

changes to the archive are traceable,

and accountability does not depend on narrative or advocacy.

Update log

This folder may contain short update notes such as:

“Dataset indexed”

“April 2024 correspondence uploaded”

“No new material added; indexing refined”

These entries are for record-keeping only.

If you are looking for raw evidence

Raw source material (including original .eml email files) is located in the evidence folders, not here.

Refer to:

01_EVIDENCE_RECORD (or equivalent)
for primary, unaltered data.

If you are looking for court documents

Judicial filings and orders are located in the court record folders.

Refer to:

pleadings,

acknowledgments,

replies,

and court decisions.
