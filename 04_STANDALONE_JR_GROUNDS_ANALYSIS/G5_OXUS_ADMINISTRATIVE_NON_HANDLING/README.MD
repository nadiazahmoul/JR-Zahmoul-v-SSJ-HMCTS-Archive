README — Standalone Ground: OXUS Disclosure and Administrative Non-Handling

Issue focus
This folder concerns the administrative and procedural handling of OXUS-related disclosure issues, including alleged failures to investigate, assess, or respond to material raised.

Purpose
The materials explore whether:

relevant disclosures were properly escalated or addressed,

administrative bodies failed to engage with regulatory or evidential implications,

and whether omissions contributed to procedural unfairness.

Status

This ground is under active analysis and has not yet been finalised.

Nothing in this folder forms part of the live JR pleadings.

Relationship to JR
OXUS is treated as a distinct but related issue, sharing common attributes with other grounds: disclosure integrity, administrative handling, and the cumulative impact on participation and fairness.
