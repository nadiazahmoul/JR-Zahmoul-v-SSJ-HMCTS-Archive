README — Standalone Ground: Withdrawal of FPR Part 3A Participation Measures

Issue focus
This folder addresses the withdrawal or non-continuation of participation measures under FPR Part 3A, including the absence of a lawful review, reasons, or recorded decision.

Purpose
The materials analyse whether:

participation measures were withdrawn without due process,

the court and/or HMCTS failed to apply the Part 3A framework correctly,

and whether the Claimant’s disabilities were properly accounted for post-trial.

Status

This is a standalone analytical ground, prepared for later consolidation.

No document in this folder currently forms part of the live pleadings.

Relationship to JR
This ground is analytically distinct from MPS but shares common attributes: participation, foreseeability, and equality of arms under Article 6 ECHR.
