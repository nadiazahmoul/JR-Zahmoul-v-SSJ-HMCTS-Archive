README — Coinbase: Judicial Review Narrative (Procedural Fairness)

Purpose of this folder
This folder contains a Judicial Review–specific narrative addressing the Coinbase issue as a matter of process, not merits, including:

failure to apply the correct analytical framework to material disclosure,

procedural unfairness and inequality of arms,

and consequences for effective participation under Article 6 ECHR.

Scope
The documents here:

do not seek to re-litigate the financial remedy outcome,

do not invite the Administrative Court to substitute findings,

and are framed strictly in public-law terms.

Status

These are standalone narrative drafts, prepared for strategic review.

They are not yet pleaded and are not part of the live JR record.

Any future reliance would occur only through formal consolidation and amendment.

Relationship to other sub-folders

This narrative is derived from the findings in 01_FINDINGS/ and the analysis in 02_LEGAL_ANALYSIS/.

It is designed to align with, but remain distinct from, appellate grounds concerning Coinbase.
