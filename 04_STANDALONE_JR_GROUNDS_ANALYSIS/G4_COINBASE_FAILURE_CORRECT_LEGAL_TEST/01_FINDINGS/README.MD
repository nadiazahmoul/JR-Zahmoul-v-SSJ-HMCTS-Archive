README — Coinbase: Extracted Judicial Findings

Purpose of this folder
This folder contains verbatim and neutral extracts of the judge’s findings relating to the Coinbase asset, including findings on value, timing, knowledge, and disclosure.

Scope
Materials here are limited to:

what the judge found,

where those findings appear in the judgment,

and how they are framed in the judgment itself.

No legal argument, critique, or reframing is included in this folder.

Status

These documents are not pleadings.

They are foundational factual material used to support later legal analysis.

They are maintained separately to avoid conflating findings with argument.

Relationship to other sub-folders

Legal evaluation of these findings is undertaken in 02_LEGAL_ANALYSIS/.

Public-law and JR framing based on these findings is undertaken in 03_JR_NARRATIVE/.
