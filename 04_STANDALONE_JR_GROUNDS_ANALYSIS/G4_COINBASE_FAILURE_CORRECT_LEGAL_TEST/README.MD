README — Standalone Ground: Coinbase Disclosure and Misapplication of Legal Test

Issue focus
This folder addresses the mischaracterisation of a serious disclosure breach relating to the Coinbase asset, including failure to apply the correct legal test to a knowing misstatement.

Purpose
The materials analyse:

findings of knowledge, timing, and materiality,

the downgrading of the conduct to “late disclosure”,

and the procedural consequences of that mischaracterisation.

Status

This is a standalone legal analysis, aligned with appellate Ground 18 but reframed for public-law scrutiny.

It is not yet consolidated into the JR pleadings.

Relationship to JR
The issue is relied upon to demonstrate:

failure to apply the correct analytical framework,

distortion of evidential control,

and procedural unfairness under Article 6, without inviting re-litigation of merits.
