README — Coinbase: Legal Analysis of Disclosure and Characterisation

Purpose of this folder
This folder contains legal analysis of the judge’s treatment of the Coinbase disclosure, focusing on:

error of characterisation (late disclosure vs knowing misstatement),

failure to apply the correct legal test,

inadequacy of reasons,

and materiality to outcome and costs.

Scope
Documents here analyse:

how the law on disclosure, statements of truth, and intent applies to the findings,

where the reasoning departs from orthodox principles,

and why that departure is legally significant.

Status

These materials are analytical working documents.

They are not relied upon in any live pleading.

They are intended for potential later consolidation into appellate or JR grounds.

Relationship to other sub-folders

The analysis is grounded in the extracted findings in 01_FINDINGS/.

The JR-specific public-law framing derived from this analysis is located in 03_JR_NARRATIVE/.
