README — Standalone Ground: Unlawful Withdrawal / Non-Enforcement of MPS

Issue focus
This folder contains analytical materials relating to the unlawful non-enforcement and effective withdrawal of Maintenance Pending Suit (MPS), treated as a participation-preserving mechanism in the underlying proceedings.

Purpose
The materials here support a standalone public-law ground concerning:

administrative failure to enforce or manage MPS,

absence of a lawful decision, record, or audit trail,

and the resulting loss of subsistence and effective participation.

Status

These documents are not pleadings.

They are not relied upon unless and until formally consolidated into the Grounds of Claim.

The operative pleadings remain those filed under 03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS.

Relationship to JR
This issue underpins Ground 1 of the Amended Grounds of Claim and provides factual and analytical depth for causation, foreseeability, and Article 6 prejudice.
