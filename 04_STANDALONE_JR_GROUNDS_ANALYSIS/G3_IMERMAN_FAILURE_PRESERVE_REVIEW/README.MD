README — Standalone Ground: Failure to Preserve and Review Imerman Material

Issue focus
This folder concerns the failure to preserve, control, and forensically review electronic devices held as Imerman material, despite their known evidential significance.

Purpose
The materials examine:

loss of institutional custody and chain-of-control,

absence of forensic review despite expert-identified relevance,

and the resulting irreversible evidential deprivation.

Status

These documents are analytical and evidential in nature.

They are not currently pleaded as a discrete JR ground, though aspects overlap with existing Grounds.

Relationship to JR
This ground supports Article 6 arguments concerning:

loss of access to primary evidence,

procedural unfairness,

and inequality of arms, particularly when read alongside the Coinbase and MPS issues.
