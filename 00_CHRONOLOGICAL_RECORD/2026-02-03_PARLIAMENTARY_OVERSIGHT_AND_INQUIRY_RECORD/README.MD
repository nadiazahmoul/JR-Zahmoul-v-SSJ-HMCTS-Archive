## Parliamentary Oversight and Inquiry Record — 3 February 2026

This folder contains a chronological, evidential record prepared to support parliamentary oversight in relation to matters arising from ongoing judicial and administrative proceedings.

The materials collected here are presented neutrally and without conclusion, for the purpose of enabling informed scrutiny by Members of Parliament, select committees, and other oversight bodies. The record focuses on documented procedural history, decision-making gaps, and evidential extracts relevant to public interest considerations.

No inference is invited beyond what is supported by the underlying documents. The folder is maintained as a transparent reference record and may be supplemented as further information becomes available.
