Purpose of this folder

This folder contains transparency notices and explanatory materials published by the Claimant to explain the public availability of documents in this repository.

The materials here are intended to:

explain the scope and purpose of disclosure,

document redaction and publication principles,

support open justice and public understanding,

and distinguish between court-filed material and publicly shared contextual material.

Documents in this folder are not pleadings and do not form part of the evidential record unless expressly stated.
