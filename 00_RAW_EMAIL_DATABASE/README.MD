
## RAW_EMAIL_DATABASE

### Purpose

This folder contains the **raw primary evidence** for the case
**Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS (AC-2025-LON-004293)**.

The database consists solely of **original `.eml` email files**.

---

### What this folder contains

* Original email files in **`.eml` format**
* Full email headers preserved (including Date, Message-ID, routing information)
* Email bodies and attachments preserved where present
* Emails organised **by month and direction (INCOMING / OUTGOING)** for ease of navigation

These files constitute the **authoritative evidential record**.

---

### What this folder does *not* contain

* No annotations
* No highlighting or tagging
* No summaries or interpretations
* No selection of “key” emails
* No legal analysis or conclusions

Any indexes, tables, summaries, or analyses derived from this material are stored **elsewhere** in the repository and are clearly labelled as derivative.

---

### Filenames and organisation

* Filenames have been **standardised for consistency and uniqueness**
* Filenames are **not evidential**
* Folder names and filenames are provided **solely to aid navigation**

The **evidential identity** of each email is defined by its internal headers (e.g. Message-ID, Date, From, To), which remain unchanged.

---

### Integrity and handling

* Emails are published in their original format
* No content has been altered, edited, or redacted
* Any mechanical processing (such as indexing or renaming) has been performed **without modifying email content or headers**

This structure allows any independent reviewer to reconstruct the correspondence chronologically and verify the record directly from the raw data.

---

### How to use this database

1. Select the relevant **month and direction**
2. Open the `.eml` files using any standard email viewer
3. Rely on the internal headers for authoritative timing and identity

No additional context is required to read the underlying correspondence.

---

### Status

This folder is intended to function as a **stable, auditable evidential record**.
Any later additions or supplements are logged separately and do not replace the original material.
