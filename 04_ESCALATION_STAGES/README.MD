Purpose of this folder

This folder records the formal escalation of concerns arising from the proceedings where ordinary court processes failed to provide effective redress.

It documents steps taken to raise issues of administrative failure, procedural unfairness, discrimination, or systemic concern with appropriate oversight bodies, public authorities, and accountability mechanisms outside the routine litigation process.

The materials in this folder provide a contemporaneous record of how concerns were:

identified,

formally raised,

pursued through escalation,

and responded to (or not responded to) by relevant institutions.

Scope of contents

Documents in this folder may include:

Formal complaints to HMCTS or the Ministry of Justice

Correspondence with oversight or investigatory bodies

Escalation letters where court processes were ineffective

Responses (or absence of responses) from public authorities

Records demonstrating exhaustion of internal remedies

These materials are retained to evidence:

procedural exhaustion,

transparency and good-faith engagement,

and the broader accountability context relevant to the proceedings.

Important distinction

This folder does not contain:

pleadings or applications filed in court,

written arguments or skeletons,

or primary evidence bundles.

Those materials are recorded separately in:

01_EVIDENCE_RECORD,

02_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS, and

03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS.

Organisational note

Documents are organised by escalation pathway and institutional recipient, rather than by court stage or chronology.
