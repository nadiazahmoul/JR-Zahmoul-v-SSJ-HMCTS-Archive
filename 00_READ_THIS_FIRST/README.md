## Judicial Review AC-2025-LON-004293 – Public Record Repository

# Read This First

This repository preserves the record relating to the Judicial Review
**Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS**
(Administrative Court: AC-2025-LON-004293).

It is structured to ensure **clarity, transparency, and auditability**.
Different types of material are deliberately separated so that evidence,
court decisions, and advocacy are not conflated.

---

## How this repository is organised

### 01_EVIDENCE_RECORD
Contains the **canonical evidential record**.

This includes:
- primary source materials (e.g. medical and clinical records),
- evidential work products (e.g. criminal referrals, Imerman materials,
  PAP correspondence, administrative handling records),
- formal exhibit bundles and indexes.

These materials exist independently of court interpretation or advocacy.

---

### 02_COURT_DECISIONS_AND_ORDERS
Contains **court-issued outputs** only.

This includes:
- orders,
- directions,
- judgments,
- procedural decisions issued by the courts.

These documents represent institutional responses to the evidential record
and the parties’ pleadings.

---

### 03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS
Contains **formal pleadings and advocacy materials**.

This includes:
- sealed claim forms and applications,
- submissions,
- skeleton arguments,
- annexes and supporting schedules.

These documents reflect the parties’ legal positions and procedural steps.

---

## How to read the record

Readers unfamiliar with the case are encouraged to proceed in this order:

1. **01_EVIDENCE_RECORD** – what exists in reality (facts and source material)
2. **02_COURT_DECISIONS_AND_ORDERS** – what the court did in response
3. **03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS** – how the issues were framed

This sequence allows independent assessment of how evidence was handled
and how decisions were reached.

---

## Notes on access and sensitivity

Some materials are sealed, redacted, or subject to legal or privacy
constraints. Filenames and folder placement reflect this where applicable.

This repository is maintained to preserve an accurate and intelligible
record. No document should be assumed to represent findings of fact unless
it is a court-issued decision.

For an overview of what material exists and where it is located, see the Consolidated Accountability Record.
