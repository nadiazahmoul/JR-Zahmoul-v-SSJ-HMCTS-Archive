## Judicial Review AC-2025-LON-004293 – Public Record Repository

This repository preserves the documentary record relating to the judicial review Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS (AC-2025-LON-004293).

Its purpose is transparency, public accountability, and preservation of the administrative record.

Reliance at the permission stage
For the avoidance of doubt, at the current permission stage, reliance is placed on documents identified in:
- 05_PLEADINGS_AND_FORMAL_FILINGS
- 05_JR_Procedural_Service_Urgency

All other folders are retained to preserve the historical, administrative, and oversight record and are not relied upon at the permission stage unless expressly stated.
