# Pre-Action Protocol (PAP) Correspondence and Notice

This folder contains Pre-Action Protocol correspondence exchanged prior to
the issue of proceedings, including letters before claim and any follow-up
communications.

These documents are preserved as **evidence of notice, forewarning, and
administrative engagement (or non-engagement)** by the relevant public
authorities.

PAP correspondence is treated here as an **evidential work product** because
it derives from, and references, primary source material while documenting
the opportunity afforded to the decision-maker to address the matters raised
before litigation.

This folder may include:
- Letters before claim
- PAP follow-up letters
- Any responses received
- Records evidencing silence or failure to respond

The documents are stored in their original form and are not pleadings or
court decisions. Court filings and judicial outputs are preserved separately
in the relevant top-level folders.
