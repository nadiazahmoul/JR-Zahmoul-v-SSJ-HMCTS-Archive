Purpose of this folder

This folder contains analytical evidential work products prepared to assist in understanding the sequence, timing, and cumulative impact of events reflected in the primary evidence.

The documents here are derived from raw primary evidence but are not themselves contemporaneous records. They function as structured explanatory tools.

Status of documents in this folder

Documents in this folder are classified as:

Evidential work products
(analytical materials; permission-stage / explanatory; referenced but not yet exhibited)

Accordingly:

they may be referenced in pleadings, applications, and supplementary notes;

they are not formal exhibit bundles unless and until the court directs otherwise;

they preserve a clear distinction between analysis and source evidence.

Typical contents

This folder may include, for example:

chronologies and timelines;

enforcement or participation analyses;

correspondence frequency or pattern analyses;

side-by-side comparisons of draft and final orders;

structured summaries linking evidence to pleaded issues.

All documents are date-anchored and named using ISO-date conventions for clarity and auditability.

Relationship to other folders

Underlying source documents relied upon are stored in:
01_EVIDENCE_RECORD / 1_RAW_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE

Formal exhibit bundles, once directed or filed, are stored in:
01_EVIDENCE_RECORD / 3_FORMAL_EXHIBIT_BUNDLES

This folder operates as an intermediate analytical layer between raw evidence and formal exhibits.

Procedural note

Inclusion of a document in this folder does not imply judicial endorsement or admission into evidence.
Its purpose is explanatory and organisational, pending directions from the court.
