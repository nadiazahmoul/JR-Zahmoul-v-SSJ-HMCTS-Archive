
# **Explanatory_Notes**

## Purpose of this folder

This folder contains **concise explanatory notes** prepared to provide **contextual linkage and clarification** between primary records, timelines, and change logs in the proceedings in *Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service* (AC-2025-LON-004293).

These notes are intended to explain **how different strands of the record relate to one another**, particularly where understanding depends on participation context, sequencing, or procedural circumstances. They do not advance legal argument or challenge the merits of any judicial determination.

---

## What this folder contains

Documents in this folder may include:

* short explanatory notes linking change logs to participation timelines;
* contextual explanations of order-drafting processes;
* notes clarifying the practical or procedural significance of identified changes;
* explanatory material addressing participation, safeguarding, or process integrity.

Each note is scoped, time-bound where appropriate, and written in neutral, descriptive language.

---

## Status and use

* The documents in this folder are **evidential work products**, not pleadings and not formal exhibits unless and until expressly relied upon.
* They may be used to:

  * assist the Court in understanding procedural context;
  * clarify how different evidential materials interrelate;
  * support accurate reading of timelines and change logs;
  * provide explanatory background for oversight or review processes.

These notes are not intended to substitute for submissions or skeleton arguments.
