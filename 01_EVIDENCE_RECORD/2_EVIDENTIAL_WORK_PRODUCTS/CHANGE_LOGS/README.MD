
# **Change_Logs**

## Purpose of this folder

This folder contains **structured change logs** documenting **material differences between successive drafts, sealed orders, or procedural instruments** in the proceedings in *Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service* (AC-2025-LON-004293).

The purpose of these documents is to provide **clear, neutral before-and-after comparisons**, identifying **what changed, when, and how**, without advancing argument or attributing motive.

---

## What this folder contains

Documents in this folder may include:

* side-by-side comparisons of draft and final orders;
* logs of substantive amendments introduced during order-drafting phases;
* change records relating to financial provisions, participation measures, or safeguards;
* version-controlled summaries identifying the point at which specific provisions were introduced, removed, or altered.

Each change log is scoped to a defined period and identifies the relevant draft numbers, dates, or instruments reviewed.

---

## Status and use

* The documents in this folder are **evidential work products**, not pleadings and not formal exhibits unless and until expressly relied upon.
* They are prepared to:

  * assist in understanding procedural and drafting history;
  * support accurate chronology and process review;
  * clarify how material provisions evolved over time;
  * provide transparency regarding unilateral or sequential amendments.

The change logs do **not** assess the merits or correctness of any judicial determination.
