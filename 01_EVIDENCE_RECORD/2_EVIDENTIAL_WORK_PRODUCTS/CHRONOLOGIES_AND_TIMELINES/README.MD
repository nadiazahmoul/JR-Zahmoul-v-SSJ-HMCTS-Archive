# **Chronologies_and_Timelines**

## Purpose of this folder

This folder contains **chronological records and timelines** prepared to assist in understanding the **sequence of events, procedural steps, and participation context** relevant to the proceedings in *Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service* (AC-2025-LON-004293).

The materials in this folder are **evidential work products**, derived from contemporaneous correspondence, court records, draft and sealed orders, and other primary sources. They are intended to provide **temporal clarity and contextual structure**, rather than to advance argument or legal submissions.
