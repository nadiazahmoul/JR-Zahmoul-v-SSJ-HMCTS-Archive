Imerman Materials and Chain of Custody
Purpose of this folder

This folder contains materials relating to the handling, custody, disclosure, and forensic treatment of electronic devices and data arising in the proceedings connected with
Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service (AC-2025-LON-004293).

The materials are preserved to maintain a clear evidential record of how electronic material was identified, held, transmitted, and referenced, including matters commonly described as Imerman material.

What this folder contains

Documents in this folder may include:

indexed Imerman exhibit bundles;

timelines relating to device custody and disclosure steps;

correspondence with solicitors concerning electronic material;

master exhibit tables and cross-reference documents;

records relevant to chain of custody and evidential integrity.

The contents document what material existed, how it was handled, and when, without asserting conclusions as to legality or outcome.

Status and scope

The materials in this folder are primary evidential records, not pleadings and not analytical work products.

Their inclusion does not invite the Administrative Court to adjudicate issues of disclosure, contempt, or criminal liability, unless and until such matters are expressly raised.

The folder exists to preserve forensic transparency, auditability, and chronology concerning electronic evidence.

No inference should be drawn as to:

the admissibility of any material,

compliance or non-compliance with disclosure obligations,

or the outcome of any related proceedings.

Relationship to other folders

Analytical timelines and explanatory notes derived from this material are stored in:

01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/2_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS

Criminal referrals referencing Imerman material (if any) are stored in:

01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/4_CRIMINAL_REFERRALS_AND_TRANSMISSIONS

Judicial Review pleadings and submissions are stored in:

This folder is maintained separately to avoid conflation between evidential custody issues and the scope of the Judicial Review.

Naming convention

Files and sub-folders use structured, date-led naming to preserve chronology, for example:

E1_YYYY-MM-DD_Description.pdf
IMERMAN_Timeline_YYYY-MM-DD.pdf
MASTER_EXHIBIT_INDEX_vX.pdf
