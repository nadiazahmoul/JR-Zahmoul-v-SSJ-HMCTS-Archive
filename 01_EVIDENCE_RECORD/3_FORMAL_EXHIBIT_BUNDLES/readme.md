Purpose of this folder

This folder contains formal exhibit bundles prepared for filing or use in court pursuant to judicial directions or procedural requirements.

Documents placed here represent materials that have been formally assembled, paginated, and designated as exhibits for the purposes of litigation.

Status of documents in this folder

Documents in this folder are classified as:

Formal exhibit bundles
(court-directed or court-filed evidential compilations)

Accordingly:

inclusion here reflects a procedural step, not merely organisational convenience;

bundles are prepared in accordance with applicable court rules and directions;

pagination and exhibit references are preserved and fixed.

Typical contents

This folder may include, for example:

court-ordered exhibit bundles;

indexed and paginated evidence bundles;

exhibits attached to witness statements;

materials expressly relied upon at hearings.

Relationship to other folders

Source materials originate from:
01_EVIDENCE_RECORD / 1_RAW_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE

Analytical materials informing bundle composition may be found in:
01_EVIDENCE_RECORD / 2_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS

This folder represents the formal evidential layer engaged once judicial directions are given.

Procedural note

Only documents placed in this folder pursuant to court directions or filings should be treated as formal exhibits.
Other materials in the repository should not be assumed to have exhibit status.

## Formal Exhibit Bundles and Indexes

This folder contains compiled exhibit bundles and indexes prepared for
external scrutiny, including bundles filed with the court or prepared
for counsel.

These materials organise and present evidence drawn from the canonical
evidence record. They do not constitute primary source evidence themselves.

NZ7 is the consolidated public exhibits bundle prepared for December 2025 reconsideration.
