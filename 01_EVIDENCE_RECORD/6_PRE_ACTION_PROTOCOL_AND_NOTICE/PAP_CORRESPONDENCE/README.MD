PAP Correspondence
Purpose

This folder contains the Pre-Action Protocol correspondence sent by the Claimant to the relevant public authorities prior to the commencement of Judicial Review proceedings in
Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service (AC-2025-LON-004293).

The documents evidence notice, escalation, and opportunity to respond in accordance with public law pre-action principles.

Contents

Documents in this folder may include:

letters before claim;

follow-up PAP correspondence;

escalation letters and final notices;

ministerial or safeguarding notices;

any responses received, or records of non-response.

The correspondence is preserved in chronological order and in its original form.

Status

These documents are primary procedural records, not pleadings or evidential work products.

They demonstrate compliance with the Pre-Action Protocol and engagement (or lack of engagement) by the public authorities.

Their inclusion does not expand the scope of the Judicial Review.

Naming convention

Files use ISO-style date prefixes, for example:

YYYY-MM-DD_PAP_Letter_Before_Claim.pdf
YYYY-MM-DD_PAP_Follow_Up.pdf
YYYY-MM-DD_Final_Notice.pdf
