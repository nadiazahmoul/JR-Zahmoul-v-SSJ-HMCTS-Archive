Pre-Action Protocol and Notice
Purpose of this folder

This folder contains Pre-Action Protocol (PAP) correspondence and formal notices exchanged prior to the commencement of Judicial Review proceedings in
Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service (AC-2025-LON-004293).

The materials document notice, forewarning, escalation, and opportunity to respond afforded to the relevant public authorities before litigation was issued, in accordance with public law pre-action principles.

What this folder contains

Documents in this folder may include:

letters before claim;

PAP follow-up correspondence;

escalation letters and final notices;

ministerial or safeguarding notices;

any responses received, or records evidencing non-response.

The documents are preserved in their original form and in chronological order.

Status and scope

The documents in this folder are not pleadings and not judicial decisions.

They constitute primary procedural records demonstrating compliance with the Pre-Action Protocol and engagement (or non-engagement) by the public authorities.

Their inclusion does not expand the scope of the Judicial Review but records the procedural steps taken prior to issue.

No inference should be drawn as to the merits of the claim or the outcome of proceedings.

Relationship to other folders

Judicial Review pleadings and sealed applications are stored in:

03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS

Evidential work products (chronologies, change logs, explanatory notes) are stored in:

01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/2_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS

Criminal referrals, where distinct, are stored in:

01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/4_CRIMINAL_REFERRALS_AND_TRANSMISSIONS

This folder is maintained separately to preserve the procedural integrity of the pre-action record.

Files use ISO-style date prefixes and descriptive titles, for example:
