Purpose of this folder

This folder contains all material relating to Oxus that is relied upon in Ground 3 of the Amended Grounds of Claim (2 January 2026).

The focus of this folder is not the determination of factual or criminal liability.
It records the administrative and procedural handling (or non-handling) of serious material placed before the court and HMCTS, and the absence of any lawful, recorded, or accountable response.

Scope of contents

Documents in this folder may include:

Documentary material relating to Oxus placed before the court

Sworn statements, exhibits, and contemporaneous correspondence referring to Oxus

Applications, referrals, or requests for action (including contempt-related material)

Evidence demonstrating the absence of administrative handling, referral, escalation, or audit trail

Records showing that serious material was received but not processed, addressed, or responded to by the court administration or HMCTS

Jurisdictional framing

As set out in Ground 3 of the Amended Grounds of Claim:

The Claimant does not invite the Administrative Court to determine the truth of allegations concerning Oxus.

The challenge is confined to the failure of public authorities to operate any identifiable administrative process when faced with serious material affecting the integrity of proceedings.

The absence of recorded decisions, reasons, referrals, or audit trails is relied upon as an arguable public law error.

This folder exists to evidence that failure.

Relationship to other folders

Primary evidence may also appear in 01_EVIDENCE_RECORD

Criminal referrals (where applicable) are recorded in 01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/4_CRIMINAL_REFERRALS_AND_TRANSMISSIONS

Pleadings relying on Oxus material are located in 03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS

This folder serves as the central Oxus reference point, ensuring clarity, coherence, and transparency.

Organisational note

Materials are organised by function and administrative pathway, not by allegation type.

Status
