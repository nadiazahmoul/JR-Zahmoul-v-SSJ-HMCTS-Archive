# **Criminal Referrals and Transmissions**

## Purpose of this folder

This folder contains a **record of criminal referrals and related transmissions** made by the Claimant to external authorities during the period relevant to the proceedings in
*Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HM Courts & Tribunals Service* (AC-2025-LON-004293).

The materials here document **formal acts of referral, notification, and transmission** of information to competent authorities. They are preserved for **transparency, auditability, and chronological accuracy**, and are maintained separately from Judicial Review pleadings and evidential work products.

---

## What this folder contains

Documents in this folder may include:

* formal criminal referral notices;
* indexed referral bundles (e.g. CR-1, CR-2A, CR-2B, CR-3);
* covering letters or notices of transmission;
* cross-reference documents identifying materials provided to authorities;
* records of dates, recipients, and scope of referrals.

The contents reflect **what was transmitted, when, and to whom**, without asserting outcomes or conclusions.

---

## Status and scope

* The documents in this folder are **not pleadings**, **not submissions**, and **not evidence filed in the Judicial Review** unless and until expressly relied upon.
* Their inclusion in this repository does **not invite the Administrative Court to adjudicate criminal matters**.
* The folder exists to preserve an **accurate historical record** of referrals made outside the JR process.

No inference should be drawn as to:

* the merits of any allegation,
* the existence of criminal liability,
* or the outcome of any referral.

---

## Relationship to other folders

* **Primary source materials** referenced in referrals may also appear in:

  * `01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/1_RAW_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE`
* **Judicial Review pleadings and applications** are stored in:

  * `03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS`
* **Chronologies, change logs, and explanatory notes** prepared for process clarity are stored in:

  * `01_EVIDENCE_RECORD/2_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS`

This folder is maintained **separately** to avoid conflation between criminal referrals and the scope of the Judicial Review.

---

## Naming convention

Referral bundles and documents use structured, date-led filenames, for example:

```
CR-1_YYYY-MM-DD_Description.pdf
CR-2A_YYYY-MM-DD_Description.pdf
CR-CROSSREF_Notice_and_Transmissions.md
```
