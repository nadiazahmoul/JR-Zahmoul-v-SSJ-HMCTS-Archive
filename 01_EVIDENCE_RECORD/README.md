# 01_EVIDENCE_RECORD


## Purpose of this folder

This folder contains the canonical evidential record underlying the Judicial Review
**Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS (AC-2025-LON-004293)**.

It preserves the **primary source evidence**, the **evidential work products** derived
from that evidence, and the **formal exhibit bundles** used in court and oversight
processes.

This structure is designed to:
- preserve accuracy and provenance,
- distinguish source material from analysis,
- enable public scrutiny,
- and ensure the record cannot be retrospectively distorted.

---

## Structural overview

### 1_SOURCE_EVIDENCE
Primary, contemporaneous records created independently of litigation.

Includes:
- Medical and clinical records (psychiatric, autism, GP, hospital, crisis team)
- NHS and clinician correspondence
- Official letters generated outside court pleadings
- Original administrative communications

These documents speak for themselves and form the factual substrate of the case.

---

### 2_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS
Materials created by the Claimant to organise, analyse, or transmit source evidence
to authorities.

Includes:
- Criminal referrals (UK nexus)
- Imerman evidence and indices
- Chain of custody narratives
- Cross-reference and transmission records

These documents **derive from** source evidence but do not replace it.

---

### 3_ANALYTICAL_AND_CONTEXTUAL_MATERIAL
Context-setting materials that explain sequence, impact, and causation.

Includes:
- Chronologies and timelines
- Post-trial event mapping
- Administrative action sequences

> **Key factual marker:**  
> The trial concluded on **28 February 2024**.  
> Participation measures were withdrawn and Maintenance Pending Suit payments
> stopped immediately thereafter, without a hearing, without notice, and while
> live court orders remained in force.

---

### 4_FORMAL_EXHIBIT_BUNDLES
Structured bundles used in court or provided to counsel, the court, or oversight bodies.

Includes:
- NZ-series exhibit bundles (NZ1–NZ7)
- Public and sealed exhibit compilations
- Indices corresponding to submissions and skeleton arguments

These bundles organise evidence; they do not supersede the underlying record.

---

## Relationship to other folders

- **Court decisions and orders** are preserved separately in  
  `02_COURT_DECISIONS_AND_ORDERS`
- **Pleadings and sealed applications** are preserved in  
  `03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS`

This separation ensures a clear distinction between:
- what the court decided,
- what parties submitted,
- and what the evidence independently shows.

---

## Integrity note

This evidence record is cumulative and non-destructive.
Documents are not removed once added.
Corrections are made by supplementation, not replacement.

The purpose is preservation of truth, not advocacy.

# 01 — EVIDENCE RECORD (Canonical)

This folder is the **canonical evidential record** for *Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS* (Judicial Review: **AC-2025-LON-004293**).

It is designed for **transparency, scrutiny, and forensic auditability**.  
All evidence is organised so a reviewer can distinguish between:
1) **primary source materials** (raw inputs),  
2) **evidential work products** (derived from sources, with limited analysis), and  
3) **formal exhibit bundles / indexes** (structured presentation of evidence).

> **Court outputs** (orders/judgments/directions) are stored separately in `02_COURT_DECISIONS_AND_ORDERS`.  
> **Pleadings and advocacy** (claim form, submissions, skeletons, annexes) are stored separately in `03_PLEADINGS_AND_SEALED_APPLICATIONS`.

---

## Naming, provenance, and integrity rules

1. **Do not edit primary evidence files once uploaded.**  
   Any commentary or analysis belongs in work products or separate notes.

2. **Keep original filenames where possible.**  
   If renaming is unavoidable (e.g., “scan123.pdf”), use:
   `YYYY-MM-DD_Source_Type_ShortDescription.ext`

3. **Redaction transparency:**  
   If a file is redacted, mark it clearly in the filename (e.g., `_REDACTED`, `_PublicVersion`).  
   Never overwrite an unredacted original with a redacted version.

4. **One canonical location per item.**  
   Avoid duplicates. If something must be referenced from elsewhere, reference it by path.
