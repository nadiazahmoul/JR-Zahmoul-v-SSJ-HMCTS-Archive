## Medical and Clinical Records

This folder contains raw medical and clinical source records,
including NHS correspondence, GP records, specialist reports,
and other healthcare documentation.
## Medical and Clinical Records (Raw Evidence)

This folder contains the complete chronological medical and clinical record.

Documents are primary source materials and have not been annotated,
edited, or analysed.

Chronological ordering reflects the evidential sequence of events.


Documents are stored here in their original form.
