## Raw / Primary Evidence

1_RAW_PRIMARY_EVIDENCE / README.md
Purpose of this folder

This folder contains primary source evidence generated contemporaneously with events relevant to the proceedings.

These materials constitute the original evidential record, created independently of the Judicial Review and prior to any analytical processing. They are preserved here to maintain authenticity, provenance, and auditability.

Status of documents in this folder

Documents in this folder are classified as:

Raw primary evidence
(contemporaneous, unfiltered source materials)

Accordingly:

documents are stored as originally created or received, subject only to redaction where required;

filenames preserve original dates and identifiers wherever possible;

no analytical commentary or synthesis is embedded within these files.

Typical contents

This folder may include, for example:

court orders and judgments;

sealed or unsealed applications;

correspondence with courts, parties, or public bodies;

medical or safeguarding records;

official letters, emails, and notices.

Relationship to other folders

Analytical chronologies and timelines derived from these materials are stored in:
01_EVIDENCE_RECORD / 2_EVIDENTIAL_WORK_PRODUCTS

Court-directed exhibit bundles incorporating selected documents from this folder are stored in:
01_EVIDENCE_RECORD / 3_FORMAL_EXHIBIT_BUNDLES

This folder forms the foundational evidential layer of the record.

Procedural note

Inclusion of a document in this folder does not imply that it has been admitted as evidence by any court.
It reflects preservation of the original record pending judicial consideration.
