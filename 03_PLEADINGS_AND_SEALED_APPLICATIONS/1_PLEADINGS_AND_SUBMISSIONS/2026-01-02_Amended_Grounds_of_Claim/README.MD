README — 2 January 2026

This folder contains the Claimant’s Amended Grounds of Claim filed and
served on 2 January 2026 in AC-2025-LON-004293.

The amendments clarify and consolidate the administrative and procedural
basis of the challenge at the permission stage. No new causes of action
are introduced. The filing addresses the administrative handling of
participation measures and MPS, the absence of reasons and audit trail,
the non-handling of serious material (Oxus), the handling of Imerman
devices, and the failure to consider cumulative impact.

The Amended Grounds were served contemporaneously on the Defendants.
