README — 25 November 2025

This folder contains materials sent on 25 November 2025 to the Administrative Court
in support of an urgent application under Form N463.

On this date, the Claimant submitted an urgent N463 application requesting
immediate acceptance, sealing, and urgent listing of the matter due to safeguarding,
disability, and evidence-preservation risks.

The email enclosed a single attachment titled “N463 URGENT BUNDLE – FINAL 25Nov2025.pdf”,
which contains the completed Form N463 and the accompanying urgent application bundle.
Service on the Defendants was stated to follow by separate email.

This entry records the formal initiation of urgent Administrative Court proceedings.
