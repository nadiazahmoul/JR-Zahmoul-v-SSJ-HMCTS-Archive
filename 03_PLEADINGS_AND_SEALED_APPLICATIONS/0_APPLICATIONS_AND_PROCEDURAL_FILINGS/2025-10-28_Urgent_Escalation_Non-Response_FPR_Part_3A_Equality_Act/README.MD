README — 28 October 2025

This folder contains the materials sent on 28 October 2025 as a formal escalation under the Pre-Action Protocol.

On this date, the Claimant issued an urgent escalation notice to senior leadership within the Ministry of Justice and HM Courts & Tribunals Service, citing the absence of any acknowledgment or response to the Pre-Action Protocol correspondence dated 20 and 21 October 2025.

The escalation was copied to the Court of Appeal, the Judicial Office, the Autism Act 2009 Committee, and circulated more widely for transparency.

The attachments include the original Pre-Action Protocol materials, the follow-up letter on participation measures, the Court of Appeal application bundle (CA-2024-001342), and the Claimant’s Parliamentary submission dated 24 October 2025.

These documents are preserved together as part of the chronological procedural record evidencing notice, non-response, and escalation.
