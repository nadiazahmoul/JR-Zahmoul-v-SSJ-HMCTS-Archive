This folder contains materials sent on 20 October 2025 by the Claimant as part of an escalation and transition point in the handling of the Oxus issue.

On this date, the Claimant sent an urgent email enclosing:

Court of Appeal materials relating to case reference CA-2024-001342, and

a formal Pre-Action Protocol letter addressed to the Ministry of Justice and HMCTS.

The Court of Appeal materials were enclosed for transparency and completeness.
No substantive appeal was pursued following this correspondence.

The Pre-Action Protocol letter placed the State on formal notice of alleged systemic procedural unfairness, Equality Act breaches, and failures under FPR Part 3A, and signalled the Claimant’s intention to pursue public-law remedies.

These documents are preserved here as part of the chronological procedural record.
