12 January 2026 — Request for Custody Clarification (Imerman Devices)

This folder records the email sent by the Claimant to Hughes Fowler Carruthers (HFC) requesting a formal custodial account of Imerman devices previously held pursuant to court process, following HFC’s statement that the material was “no longer in the possession of this firm”.

The correspondence was copied to the Defendants in the Judicial Review (AC-2025-LON-004293), the Administrative Court, and the House of Commons Justice Committee, given the public interest issues engaged.

The email references publicly available exhibits and indices preserved in the Evidence Record.
