README — 9 December 2025

This folder contains the materials filed on 9 December 2025 in support of the
Claimant’s request for reconsideration of the Order of 4 December 2025 in
AC-2025-LON-004293.

The filing comprised a consolidated reconsideration submission, skeleton argument,
safeguarding annex (medical and autism evidence), and an indexed exhibit pack.

The email filing was copied to the Secretary of State for Justice, the House of Commons
Justice Committee, and the House of Lords Autism Act Committee, and formally notified
those recipients of the existence of a public, timestamped GitHub repository
containing the official documents in this Judicial Review.

This entry records the completion of the reconsideration record and the initiation of
formal transparency and parliamentary oversight.
