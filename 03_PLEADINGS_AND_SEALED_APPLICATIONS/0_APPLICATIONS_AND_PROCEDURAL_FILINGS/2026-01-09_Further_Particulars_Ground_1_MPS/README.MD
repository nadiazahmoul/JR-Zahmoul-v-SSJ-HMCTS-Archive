README — Further Particulars of Ground 1 (MPS)

Case: Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS
Administrative Court reference: AC-2025-LON-004293

Purpose of this folder

This folder contains a permission-stage procedural filing submitted to the Administrative Court in support of Ground 1 of the Claimant’s Amended Grounds of Claim.

The documents here seek permission to rely on further particulars clarifying the administrative and procedural basis of Ground 1, namely the unlawful non-enforcement and effective withdrawal of Maintenance Pending Suit (MPS) as a participation-preserving measure.

Procedural status

These documents do not introduce any new cause of action.

They do not amend or replace the Amended Grounds of Claim dated 2 January 2026.

They are filed solely to assist the Court at the permission stage, by clarifying chronology, administrative handling, and causation.
