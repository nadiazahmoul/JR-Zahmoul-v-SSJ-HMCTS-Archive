README — 29 October 2025 (Stage 2 Escalation)

This folder contains materials sent on 29 October 2025 as a formal Stage 2 escalation under the HMCTS Complaints and Escalation Policy.

On this date, the Claimant issued a second, distinct email escalating matters to senior leadership within the Ministry of Justice and HM Courts & Tribunals Service. The correspondence alleged that Pre-Action Protocol notices dated 20 and 21 October 2025 had been misclassified as a judicial complaint and that no substantive engagement had occurred.

The email enclosed a formal Stage 2 Escalation Letter dated 29 October 2025, together with the full set of prior Pre-Action, follow-up, and escalation correspondence, and the Claimant’s submission to the Autism Act 2009 Committee.

These documents are preserved together to reflect the complete escalation pack provided at Stage 2 and to record senior-level notice and widening public and parliamentary oversight.
