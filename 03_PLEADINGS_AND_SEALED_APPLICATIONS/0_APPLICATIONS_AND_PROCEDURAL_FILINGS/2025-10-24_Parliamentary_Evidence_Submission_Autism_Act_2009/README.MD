README — 24 October 2025

This folder contains the materials submitted on 24 October 2025 as formal evidence to the House of Lords Autism Act 2009 Committee.

On this date, the Claimant submitted a consolidated evidence pack addressing an enforcement gap under the Autism Act 2009 and the Family Procedure Rules Part 3A, using live judicial proceedings as a case study.

The submission included:
• a covering letter to the Chair of the Committee dated 24 October 2025;
• the Pre-Action Protocol Letter dated 20 October 2025 and Annex A;
• a Follow-Up Pre-Action Letter dated 21 October 2025 concerning the unlawful revocation of participation measures;
• an application and evidence bundle relating to CA-2024-001342, relied upon as procedural evidence; and
• a ministerial letter dated 5 August 2025 acknowledging the absence of statutory enforcement powers under the Autism Act 2009.

These documents are preserved together to reflect the materials provided to Parliament at that time and their role in the chronological procedural record.
