README — 15 December 2025 (Third Email)

This folder contains correspondence sent on 15 December 2025 to the Clerk
of the House of Commons Justice Committee notifying the Committee that a
Judicial Review was filed on 1 December 2025 (AC-2025-LON-004293)
concerning administrative failures by MoJ/HMCTS in the handling of
criminal evidence.

The email enclosed the press release and the unanswered Pre-Action
Protocol Letter dated 19 November 2025 and provided a direct link to the
public GitHub repository containing the criminal referrals, transmission
history, and supporting material.

The correspondence was sent for the Committee’s awareness only and did
not request specific action.
