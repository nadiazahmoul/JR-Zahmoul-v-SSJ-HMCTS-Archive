README — 15 December 2025

This folder contains materials sent on 15 December 2025 notifying the
Defendants that the Judicial Review filed on 1 December 2025
(AC-2025-LON-004293) is now a matter of public record.

The email provided formal notice that the Pre-Action Protocol Letter dated
19 November 2025 remains unanswered and that the evidential record
underpinning the claim has been placed in the public domain for
transparency and scrutiny.

The correspondence enclosed a press release and referenced a public
GitHub repository containing lawfully publishable documents, including
criminal-referral material, transmission history, and supporting evidence.
This entry marks the transition of the matter into the public-interest
domain.
