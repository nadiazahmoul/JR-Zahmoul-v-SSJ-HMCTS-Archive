README — 18 December 2025

This folder contains correspondence sent on 18 December 2025 to the
Administrative Court providing an update following the Claimant’s
Reconsideration Submission filed on 9 December 2025 in
AC-2025-LON-004293.

The email notified the Court of material developments relevant to
safeguarding and administrative risk, including the public transmission
of criminal evidence, unresolved chain-of-custody issues concerning
Imerman devices, and the continuing necessity of an oral and public
permission hearing.

The correspondence was copied to the House of Commons Justice Committee
and the House of Lords Autism Act 2009 Committee for transparency.
