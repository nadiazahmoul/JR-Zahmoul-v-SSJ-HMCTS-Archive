README — 19 November 2025

This folder contains the Pre-Action Protocol Letter (Judicial Review) served on
19 November 2025.

On this date, the Claimant served a formal Letter Before Claim under the
Pre-Action Protocol for Judicial Review on the Secretary of State for Justice,
the Permanent Secretary of the Ministry of Justice, and the Chief Executive of
HM Courts & Tribunals Service.

The Pre-Action Protocol letter concerns alleged failures to:
• safeguard, log, process, and transmit serious criminal evidence;
• preserve and review the Imerman devices containing source-level criminal evidence;
• comply with Equality Act 2010 duties and the Public Sector Equality Duty; and
• uphold procedural fairness and the duty of candour.

The covering email states that Exhibit A (a single exhibit in sub-parts A-1 to A-5)
would be transmitted in separate emails due to file size limits.

A substantive response was required by 16:00 on 24 November 2025. This entry
marks the formal commencement of the Judicial Review pre-action process.
