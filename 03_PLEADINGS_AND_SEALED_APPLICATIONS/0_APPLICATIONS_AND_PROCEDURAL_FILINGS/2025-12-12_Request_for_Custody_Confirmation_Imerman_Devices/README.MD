README — 12 December 2025

This folder contains correspondence sent on 12 December 2025 requesting formal
confirmation of the custody status of electronic devices identified as “Imerman
material” and delivered to Hughes Fowler Carruthers on 13 April 2021.

The email was sent in the context of a live Judicial Review
(AC-2025-LON-004293) concerning the administrative handling of court-controlled
evidence and was copied to the Ministry of Justice, HMCTS Legal, the Private
Secretary to the Lord Chancellor, Withers LLP, and the House of Commons Justice
Committee.

The correspondence requested confirmation of whether the devices remain in
custody, details of any release (including date and recipient), the court order or
authority relied upon, and a full audit trail of movements or access.

This entry records the formal initiation of a chain-of-custody inquiry.
