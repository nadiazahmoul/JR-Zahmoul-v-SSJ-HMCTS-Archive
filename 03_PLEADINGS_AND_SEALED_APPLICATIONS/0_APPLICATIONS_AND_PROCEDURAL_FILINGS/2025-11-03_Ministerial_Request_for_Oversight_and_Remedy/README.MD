README — 3 November 2025

This folder contains materials sent on 3 November 2025 to the Secretaries of State for Justice and for Health and Social Care requesting ministerial oversight and remedy for systemic breaches of the Equality Act 2010 and Family Procedure Rules Part 3A.

The correspondence followed the conclusion of a staged administrative escalation (20–31 October 2025) in which no substantive response was provided by HMCTS or the Ministry of Justice.

The submission enclosed:
• a formal Ministerial Letter dated 3 November 2025;
• the Court of Appeal application bundle (CA-2024-001342);
• Minister Stephen Kinnock MP’s August 2025 evidence to the Autism Act 2009 Committee concerning the absence of enforcement powers; and
• the consolidated Stage 3 Escalation Bundle dated 31 October 2025.

This entry marks the transition from administrative escalation to direct ministerial accountability and parliamentary scrutiny.
