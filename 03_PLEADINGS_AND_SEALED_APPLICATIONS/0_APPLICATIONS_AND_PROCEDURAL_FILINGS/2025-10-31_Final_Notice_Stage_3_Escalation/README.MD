### 31 October 2025 — Final Notice & Stage 3 Escalation (Equality Act / FPR Part 3A)

On 31 October 2025, the Applicant issued a Final Notice and Stage 3 Escalation to HMCTS and the Ministry of Justice concerning continuing administrative failures to comply with Equality Act 2010 duties and Family Procedure Rules Part 3A.

This step followed repeated non-response and misclassification of Pre-Action correspondence raising unlawful revocation of participation measures, discriminatory mischaracterisation of disability-related behaviour, and the absence of any enforcement mechanism for vulnerable court users.

The attached Stage 3 Escalation Bundle consolidates all correspondence and evidential material exchanged between 20 and 31 October 2025 and formally places the Respondent public authorities on notice. No substantive response was received within the stated deadline.

This entry marks the conclusion of the October 2025 escalation sequence and directly precedes the initiation of Judicial Review proceedings.
