README — 29 October 2025

This folder contains correspondence dated 29 October 2025 relating to HMCTS’s handling of a complaint connected to CA-2024-001342.

On this date, HMCTS (RCJ Civil Appeals Office) issued an email acknowledging receipt of the Claimant’s Pre-Action Protocol correspondence dated 20, 21, and 28 October 2025, stating that the Court of Appeal process was at an end and responding under the HMCTS complaints procedure.

In the same email thread, the Claimant replied to clarify that the complaint concerned HMCTS administrative and service failures under the Equality Act 2010 and FPR Part 3A, not a challenge to judicial decisions. The Claimant requested escalation to a Stage 2 review by the Operations Manager and set out specific questions requiring response.

The reply re-attached the Pre-Action Protocol Letter (20 October 2025), the Follow-Up Letter on participation measures (21 October 2025), and the Urgent Escalation notice (28 October 2025). These documents are preserved together as part of the chronological procedural record.
