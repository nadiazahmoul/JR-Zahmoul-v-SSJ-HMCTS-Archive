README — 10 November 2025

This folder contains materials sent on 10 November 2025 requesting referral of a complaint to the Parliamentary and Health Service Ombudsman (PHSO).

On this date, the Claimant wrote to her Member of Parliament, Joe Powell MP, requesting that he sign and refer a completed PHSO complaint under the Parliamentary Commissioner Act 1967.

The email enclosed:
• a completed PHSO Complaint Form dated 9 November 2025; and
• a paginated Comprehensive Evidence Submission concerning administrative and equality-law failures by the Ministry of Justice and HM Courts & Tribunals Service.

The correspondence followed exhaustion of administrative escalation and ministerial correspondence routes and represents escalation to statutory ombudsman oversight. The Claimant indicated that supplementary materials would follow due to health constraints.
