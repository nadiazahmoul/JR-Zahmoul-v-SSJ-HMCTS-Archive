README — 30 October 2025

This folder contains materials sent on 30 October 2025 as a Stage 3 escalation to executive leadership within the Ministry of Justice and HM Courts & Tribunals Service.

On this date, the Claimant issued a Stage 3 escalation email concerning continued non-response and misclassification of Pre-Action correspondence raising administrative and equality-law breaches under FPR Part 3A and the Equality Act 2010.

The email enclosed a single compiled submission dated 30 October 2025, comprising:
• the Stage 3 Escalation Letter;
• Annex B (Background Note on misclassification of equality-law breaches as judicial matters); and
• an indexed set of prior correspondence and exhibits, incorporated by reference.

Earlier documents were not re-attached separately. This folder preserves the exact materials sent as part of the Stage 3 escalation and records executive-level notice and consolidation of issues.
