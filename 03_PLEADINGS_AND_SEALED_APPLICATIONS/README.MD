# Pleadings and Sealed Applications

This folder contains formal pleadings, submissions, skeleton arguments,
annexes, and sealed applications filed in the proceedings.

These documents reflect advocacy positions and procedural steps taken
by the parties. They are preserved separately from evidence and from
court decisions to maintain clarity between fact, argument, and outcome.

Purpose of this folder

This folder contains the formal pleadings, applications, submissions, skeleton arguments, and annexes filed in the Judicial Review proceedings AC-2025-LON-004293 (Zahmoul v Secretary of State for Justice & HMCTS).

Documents in this folder represent the procedural and legal record of what has been formally placed before the Court, including sealed applications, operative pleadings, and materials relied upon at the permission and substantive stages
