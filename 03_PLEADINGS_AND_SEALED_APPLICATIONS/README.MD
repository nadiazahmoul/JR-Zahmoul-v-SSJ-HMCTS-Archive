# Pleadings and Sealed Applications

It records what has been formally placed before the Court, including applications, pleadings, written arguments, and annexes that form part of the court record. Documents here represent the authoritative procedural history of the case, distinct from the underlying evidential record.

Folder structure and scope

0_APPLICATIONS_AND_PROCEDURAL_FILINGS
Applications and notices that seek procedural steps or directions from the Court (e.g. permission-stage applications, extensions of time, adjournments, applications to rely on further material).

1_PLEADINGS_AND_SUBMISSIONS
Operative statements of case defining the issues before the Court, including Grounds of Claim, Summary Grounds of Response, Replies, and associated covering letters.

2_WRITTEN_ARGUMENTS_AND_SUBMISSIONS
Written legal arguments prepared for hearings, including skeleton arguments and other submissions directed to the judge.

3_ANNEXES_TO_PLEADINGS
Annexes, schedules, chronologies, and exhibit packs expressly referenced in pleadings or submissions, incorporated by reference into the legal case.

Note:
Primary evidence is recorded separately in 01_EVIDENCE_RECORD. This folder is organised by procedural function, not chronology.
