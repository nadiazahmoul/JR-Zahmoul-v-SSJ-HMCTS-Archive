## **A_ORIENTATION — README**

### **Purpose of this section**

The **A_ORIENTATION** section provides the entry point to the Consolidated Accountability Record (CAR).

It explains **what this record is**, **how it should be read**, and **why consolidation is necessary**.
This section is designed to be read **before** any other part of the repository.

---

### **Contents of this section**

* **A1_Executive_Summary**
  A concise overview of the full record, setting out:

  * the scope of matters covered,
  * the time period involved,
  * the relationship between proceedings,
  * and the reason consolidation is required to understand cumulative impact.

Future documents in this section may include:

* a jurisdictional map and proceedings matrix,
* a summary of disability status and participation framework,
* and clarification of what this record is and is not.

---

### **How this section should be used**

This section:

* provides **context only**,
* does not advance legal argument,
* and does not seek determination of any issue.

Courts, oversight bodies, and readers are directed here to understand **why individual decisions or applications are referenced within a wider factual context**.

---

### **What this section does not do**

* It does not replace pleadings or evidence filed in court.
* It does not invite adjudication of matters outside any tribunal’s jurisdiction.
* It does not allege misconduct or wrongdoing.

Its function is **explanatory and orienting**, not adversarial.

---

### **Reading order**

Readers should:

1. Read **A1_Executive_Summary** in full.
2. Proceed to other sections of the CAR as needed for chronology or subject matter.

---

### **Status**

This section is **foundational**.
Its structure is stable, though its content may be refined for clarity as the record develops.
