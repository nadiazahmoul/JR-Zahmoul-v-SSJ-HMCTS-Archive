## **README.md — Consolidated Accountability Record (CAR)**

### **Consolidated Accountability Record (CAR)**

**Zahmoul v State / Courts / Systems (2021–Present)**

---

### **Purpose**

This repository contains the **Consolidated Accountability Record (CAR)**.

The CAR is a **public, factual, longitudinal record** bringing together documents, decisions, correspondence, and oversight materials arising from multiple interrelated proceedings involving a disabled litigant.

Its purpose is to:

* prevent fragmentation of related matters,
* enable transparency and scrutiny,
* and allow oversight bodies, courts, and observers to understand the **cumulative context** in which decisions have been taken.

---

### **What this record is**

* A **consolidated reference point** for multiple proceedings and oversight processes
* A **chronological and thematic record** of documents and decisions
* A **contextual framework** explaining how different matters intersect over time

The CAR allows each proceeding to be understood **in relation to others**, rather than in isolation.

---

### **What this record is not**

* It is **not** a court pleading
* It is **not** an appeal or application
* It is **not** an allegation of individual wrongdoing
* It does **not** ask any court or body to determine matters outside its jurisdiction

The CAR exists to **document and demonstrate**, not to argue.

---

### **How to read this repository**

* Start with **A_ORIENTATION / A1_Executive_Summary**
* Sections are organised by **topic and function**, not by forum
* Documents are placed to show **sequence, interaction, and cumulative effect**

Where relevant, individual proceedings (e.g. appeals, judicial review, complaints) may reference this record **for context only**.

---

### **Scope**

The record currently covers:

* family proceedings and post-judgment implementation,
* disability status and participation measures,
* administrative and enforcement issues,
* evidence handling and safeguarding,
* public law proceedings,
* appellate decisions,
* parliamentary and oversight correspondence.

The record spans **2021 to the present** and is updated as matters progress.

---

### **Why consolidation matters**

When matters are treated separately:

* cumulative harm can be obscured,
* responsibility can be deflected,
* and no single forum sees the full picture.

The CAR exists to ensure that decisions are understood **in context**, particularly where disability, vulnerability, and access to justice are engaged.

---

### **Status**

This is a **living record**.

The structure is fixed.
The content develops over time.
